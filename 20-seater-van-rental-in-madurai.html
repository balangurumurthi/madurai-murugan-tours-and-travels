20 Seater Van Rental in Madurai — FAQ

What is the price of a 20 seater van in Madurai?
The 20 seater van is priced on request. We quote per day with a km coverage for local and short trips, or per km for outstation trips. The final amount depends on your dates, route, total kilometres and number of days. Toll charges, parking charges, permit charges (interstate / state), hill charges (if any) and driver bata are extra. All the above charges will be extra wherever applicable.
How many people can travel in the 20 seater van?
Up to 20 passengers plus the driver. For multi-day trips with suitcases, a group of about 16–18 travels more comfortably. For bigger groups, see our 25 seater van or 33 seater mini bus.
Is the van A/C and does it come with a driver?
Yes. The van is A/C with pushback seats, luggage space and a music system, and every booking comes with an experienced driver.
Should I choose a 20 seater van or a Tempo Traveller?
For up to 12 people, a Tempo Traveller A/C 12+1 (₹5,200 per day, 100 km) is usually enough. Once your group grows past that, the 20 seater keeps everyone in one vehicle.
Can the 20 seater van go to Kodaikanal, Munnar or Kerala?
Yes. Our drivers handle the Kodaikanal and Munnar ghat roads and Kerala routes regularly. Hill charges may apply on ghat routes and an interstate permit is needed for Kerala. All the above charges will be extra wherever applicable.
Do you provide the van for weddings and functions?
Yes. The 20 seater is popular for moving wedding guests between the hall, hotel, temple and railway station. Share your schedule and we will quote for the day.
How early should I book a 20 seater van?
Book as soon as your dates are fixed. Group vans get booked early on wedding muhurtham dates, long weekends and festival days.
