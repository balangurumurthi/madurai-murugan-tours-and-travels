Corporate Car Rental — Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide GST invoices for corporate car rental?
Yes. Every corporate booking can be billed with a proper GST invoice in your company's name. Our GSTIN is 33BAQPJ1077F1ZR. Share your company name, billing address and GSTIN when you book.
Can we get monthly billing instead of paying per trip?
Monthly billing is available on request for regular corporate accounts. All trips for the month are consolidated into one statement and invoice. Tell us about your expected usage and we will discuss the billing terms.
Do you offer monthly car rental in Madurai?
Yes. We provide monthly and long-term car rental with a driver for companies, project teams and visiting executives. Monthly rental is quoted based on the vehicle, expected kilometres and working hours, so contact us with your requirement for a quote.
Do you provide employee pickup and drop service in Madurai?
Yes. We run employee pickup and drop for offices, factories, hospitals and institutions in and around Madurai, including early-morning and late-night shift timings. Depending on the number of staff we suggest sedans, Innova, Tempo Traveller, Urbania, vans or a mini bus.
Can you handle airport and railway station transfers for executives and guests?
Yes. We arrange Madurai airport and Madurai Junction railway station pickups and drops for your executives, visiting clients and guests, with name-placard pickup at the arrival exit on request. Share the flight or train details when you book.
Do you provide transport for corporate events, conferences and meetings?
Yes. For events, conferences, dealer meets, offsites and MICE groups we plan the vehicle mix — cars for VIPs plus Tempo Travellers, Force Urbania, 20 or 25 seater vans and 33 seater mini buses for delegates — and coordinate hotel–venue–airport movements.
How is corporate car rental charged?
Daily bookings follow our standard tariff — 100 km or 200 km coverage per day with an extra per-km rate, or km basis with a per-day minimum. Toll, parking, permit, driver bata and hill charges are extra wherever applicable. Monthly rental, van and mini bus requirements are quoted on request.
Is your corporate taxi service available 24x7?
Yes. Our support line is available 24x7 for bookings, changes and on-trip help, and corporate accounts get a single point of contact who handles all their bookings.
How do we get a corporate quote?
Fill in the corporate enquiry form on this page — company name, contact person, phone, requirement type, dates or duration and vehicles needed — and it opens WhatsApp with the details typed in. You can also call +91 99946 67622. We reply with a quote for your requirement.
