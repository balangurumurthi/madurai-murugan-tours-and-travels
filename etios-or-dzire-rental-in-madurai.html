Right car for your trip?

Is a sedan the right choice for you?

A quick check before you book. "Dzire or equivalent" means we send a Dzire or another 4+1 AC sedan of the same class — same seats, same boot, same fare.

The Dzire is perfect for

  • Couples and solo travellers on temple or sightseeing trips
  • Families of 3–4 with one large suitcase each
  • Madurai airport (IXM) and railway station pickups and drops
  • Business visits and corporate travel
  • One-way drops to Chennai, Coimbatore, Trichy or Bangalore
  • Madurai city darshan — Meenakshi Amman, Thiruparankundram, Azhagar Kovil

Pick a bigger car if

  • You are 5 or more travellers — try the Ertiga (from ₹4,000/day)
  • Four adults are carrying large suitcases for a multi-day trip
  • Elders in your group prefer a higher seat that is easier to get in and out of
  • You want extra room and a softer ride on long ghat drives — the Innova (from ₹4,200/day) suits best

Questions we get every day

Dzire rental in Madurai — FAQ

How much does it cost to rent a Dzire in Madurai?
The Dzire or equivalent costs ₹3,200 per day on the 100 km plan (extra km ₹11/km) or ₹4,400 per day on the 200 km plan (extra km ₹13/km). For long outstation trips you can choose km basis at ₹14/km with a minimum of 300 km per day. Toll charges, parking charges, permit charges, driver bata and hill charges are extra wherever applicable.
What does "Dzire or equivalent" mean?
We send a Maruti Suzuki Dzire or another 4+1 seater AC sedan of the same size and comfort, depending on which car is free on your date. The tariff, seating and luggage space are the same either way. If you need one specific model, tell us when you book and we will confirm availability.
How many passengers and how much luggage fit in the Dzire?
The Dzire seats 4 passengers plus the driver. Its separate boot takes about two large suitcases and a couple of soft bags. If you are four adults with big suitcases on a multi-day trip, an Ertiga or Innova will be more comfortable.
Is the Dzire good for Madurai airport and railway station transfers?
Yes, it is the car most people book for transfers. We pick up from Madurai airport (IXM), Madurai Junction railway station, Mattuthavani and Periyar bus stands and city hotels. Share your flight or train number on WhatsApp and the driver will time the pickup to your arrival.
Can I book the Dzire for a one-way drop?
Yes. Sedans are the most popular choice for one-way drops from Madurai to places like Chennai, Coimbatore, Trichy and Bangalore. One-way trips are quoted separately from the round-trip tariff — see our one-way taxi page or send your route on WhatsApp.
What is charged extra on a Dzire booking?
Toll charges, parking charges, permit charges, driver bata and hill charges. All the above charges will be extra wherever applicable. Everything else — the AC sedan, the driver and fuel for the kilometres in your plan — is covered in the rate.
Should I choose the Dzire or the Ertiga?
Choose the Dzire for up to 4 travellers with moderate luggage — it is ₹800 cheaper per day than the Ertiga on the 100 km plan and ₹4/km cheaper on km basis. Choose the Ertiga if you are 5 to 7 people, carry large suitcases for several days, or have elders who prefer a higher seat.
How far in advance should I book the Dzire?
For local trips and transfers a few hours' notice is often enough. For weekends, long weekends, Chithirai festival dates and Tamil New Year, please book at least 3–5 days in advance so we can hold a sedan for you.
