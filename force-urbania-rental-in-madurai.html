Force Urbania Rental in Madurai — FAQ

What is the Force Urbania rental price in Madurai?
The 12 Seater Urbania is ₹9,000 per day and the 16 Seater Urbania is ₹10,200 per day, each with 100 km coverage per day. Extra km is ₹27/km for the 12 Seater and ₹29/km for the 16 Seater. For the 200 km plan, please ask us — we quote it for your route. Toll charges, parking charges, permit charges (interstate / state), hill charges (if any) and driver bata are extra. All the above charges will be extra wherever applicable.
What is the difference between the 12 seater and 16 seater Urbania?
Both are Force Urbania vans with the same A/C, pushback-seat comfort. The 12 seater suits groups of up to 12 people; the 16 seater carries up to 16. If your group is close to 12 and carrying plenty of luggage for a multi-day trip, the 16 seater gives you breathing room.
How is the Force Urbania different from a Tempo Traveller?
The Urbania is Force Motors’ newer-generation premium van, chosen for a more premium cabin and ride. If budget matters more, our Tempo Traveller A/C 12+1 is ₹5,200 per day (100 km).
What features does the Force Urbania have?
Spacious pushback seats, A/C comfort, USB charging, a music system and ample luggage space — driven by an experienced driver from our Madurai team.
Can we take the Urbania to Kodaikanal, Munnar or Kerala?
Yes. Our drivers regularly cover Kodaikanal, Munnar, Thekkady and other Kerala routes. Hill charges may apply on ghat routes, and an interstate permit is needed for Kerala. All the above charges will be extra wherever applicable.
What is included in the Urbania day rate and what is extra?
The day rate covers the Urbania with an experienced driver and 100 km of running for the day. Toll charges, parking charges, permit charges (interstate / state), hill charges (if any) and driver bata are extra. All the above charges will be extra wherever applicable.
How do I book the Force Urbania?
Call +91 99946 67622 or +91 90879 67622, or send the booking form on this page to WhatsApp with your date, pickup point, group size and plan. We confirm the vehicle and the final amount before your trip.
