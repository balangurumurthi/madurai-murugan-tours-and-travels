Innova Crysta Rental in Madurai — FAQ

How much does it cost to rent an Innova Crysta in Madurai?
There are two per-day plans for local and outstation use: ₹4,900/day including 100 km (extra km ₹16/km) and ₹6,700/day including 200 km (extra km ₹16/km). For long outstation trips we also offer a km basis rate of ₹23/km with a minimum of 300 km per day. Toll, parking, permit, driver bata and hill charges are extra wherever applicable.
Which plan should I choose — 100 km, 200 km or km basis?
The 100 km plan suits city use such as Meenakshi temple darshan, local sightseeing and airport or railway transfers. The 200 km plan suits longer days with more driving. The km basis rate is meant for long outstation trips. Share your itinerary on WhatsApp and we'll recommend the plan that works out cheapest for you.
What is charged extra on top of the plan?
Toll charges, parking charges, permit charges (for example when the trip enters Kerala), driver bata and hill charges are not part of the plan rates. All the above charges will be extra wherever applicable.
How many people and how much luggage fit in the Innova Crysta?
The Crysta seats 7 passengers plus the driver. With every seat taken there is room for about 3–4 medium bags behind the last row; with 5–6 passengers you get noticeably more luggage space. Tell us your bag count when booking and we'll advise.
Is the Innova Crysta good for Kodaikanal and Munnar?
Yes. Its diesel engine and sturdy build handle the Kodaikanal and Munnar ghat roads comfortably with a full load, and our drivers drive these routes regularly. Hill charges and Kerala permit charges are extra wherever applicable.
Innova Crysta or Innova Hycross — which should I book?
Both seat 7 passengers plus the driver. The Crysta is rugged and proven on long routes and ghat roads, and costs less (from ₹4,900/day). The Innova Hycross (from ₹5,700/day) is newer, quieter and smoother — ideal when cabin comfort matters most.
Do you offer Madurai airport and railway station pickups in the Innova Crysta?
Yes. Send your flight or train number on WhatsApp and we'll time the pickup to your arrival at Madurai airport or Madurai Junction, then continue to your hotel or straight onto your tour.
How far in advance should I book?
For weekday local trips a few hours' notice is often enough. For weekends, long weekends and festival dates such as Chithirai, please book at least 3–5 days ahead to be sure of an Innova Crysta.
