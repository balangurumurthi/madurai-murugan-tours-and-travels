Seven adults travel without squeezing. The middle row has generous legroom, and the high seating position makes it easy for elders to step in and out at every temple stop.

Innova Rental in Madurai — FAQ

How much does it cost to rent an Innova in Madurai?
The Innova or equivalent costs ₹4,200 per day on the 100 km plan (extra km ₹13/km) or ₹5,700 per day on the 200 km plan (extra km ₹13/km). For long outstation trips you can choose km basis at ₹20/km with a minimum of 300 km per day. Toll charges, parking charges, permit charges, driver bata and hill charges are extra wherever applicable.
Is this the Innova Crysta?
No. This page is for the standard Toyota Innova or an equivalent 7+1 seater from the same class, which is the most economical Innova option. If you specifically want the newer Innova Crysta or the hybrid Innova Hycross, book those directly — they have their own pages and rates.
How many people and how much luggage fit in the Innova?
The Innova seats 7 passengers plus the driver. With all three rows in use there is room for about 3–4 medium suitcases behind the last row plus small bags in the cabin. If you are 5 or fewer with heavy luggage, the third row can be folded to free up a much bigger boot.
Should I choose the Innova or the Ertiga?
The Ertiga is ₹200 cheaper per day on the 100 km plan and works well for families of 4–6. The Innova costs a little more but has a taller, stronger body, more legroom in the middle row, a better ride on rough or ghat roads and a slightly bigger boot. For 6–7 adults, elders, or long hill trips, most families prefer the Innova.
What is charged extra on an Innova booking?
Toll charges, parking charges, permit charges, driver bata and hill charges. All the above charges will be extra wherever applicable. Everything else — the AC Innova or equivalent, the driver and fuel for the kilometres in your plan — is covered in the rate.
Is the Innova good for Kodaikanal, Munnar and other hill trips?
Yes. The Innova's diesel torque and high ground clearance make it one of the most comfortable choices for the Kodaikanal, Munnar, Thekkady and Valparai ghat roads, and our drivers do these climbs regularly. Hill charges are extra wherever applicable.
Can the Innova pick us up from Madurai airport or railway station?
Yes. We pick up from Madurai airport (IXM), Madurai Junction railway station, Mattuthavani and Periyar bus stands, and any hotel in the city. Share your flight or train number on WhatsApp and the driver will time the pickup to your arrival.
How far in advance should I book the Innova?
For weekday local trips a few hours' notice is often enough. For weekends, long weekends, Chithirai festival dates, Sabarimala season and Tamil New Year, please book at least 3–5 days in advance so we can hold an Innova for you.
