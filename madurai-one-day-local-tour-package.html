Places covered in the Madurai one day sightseeing tour

Pick the stops you like — the driver adjusts the route. Distances are approximate, from the Meenakshi Temple area.

City centre · 1.5–2 hrs

Meenakshi Amman Temple

The heart of Madurai — a vast temple to Goddess Meenakshi and Lord Sundareswarar with towering sculpted gopurams, the Golden Lotus Tank and the Hall of a Thousand Pillars. Go early in the morning for a calmer darshan.

~2 km · 45–60 min

Thirumalai Nayakkar Palace

The 17th-century palace of King Thirumalai Nayak, famous for its massive pillars, arches and grand courtyard. An evening sound-and-light show tells the palace's story — check the show timing on the day.

~5 km · 20–30 min

Vandiyur Mariamman Teppakulam

One of the largest temple tanks in Tamil Nadu, with a mandapam on an island at its centre. It comes alive during the Float Festival (Teppa Thiruvizha) in the Tamil month of Thai, around January–February.

~4 km · 45–60 min

Gandhi Memorial Museum

Housed in the historic Tamukkam Palace, the museum traces India's freedom struggle and Mahatma Gandhi's life — including the blood-stained cloth he wore on the day he was assassinated.

~1 km · 30–45 min

Koodal Azhagar Temple

An ancient Vishnu temple and one of the 108 Divya Desams, close to Periyar bus stand. Its tiered sanctum shows Lord Vishnu in seated, standing and reclining forms.

~21 km · 1.5–2 hrs with Pazhamudircholai

Alagar Kovil (Kallazhagar Temple)

A Divya Desam temple to Lord Kallazhagar at the foot of the green Alagar hills — the deity whose procession into the Vaigai is the highlight of Madurai's Chithirai festival.

~8 km · 45–60 min

Thiruparankundram Murugan Temple

The first of Lord Murugan's six abodes (Aarupadai Veedu), a rock-cut cave temple at the foot of a granite hill where Murugan is said to have married Deivanai. Planning all six? See our Aarupadai Veedu tour package.

~4 km uphill from Alagar Kovil

Pazhamudircholai Murugan Temple

The sixth abode of Lord Murugan, on the wooded Alagar hills above Alagar Kovil. Devotees usually combine both temples in one visit; the Noopura Gangai spring lies further up the hill.

Temple dress code & timings — good to know

Temple timings

Meenakshi Amman Temple is open roughly 5 AM – 12:30 PM and 4 PM – 9:30 PM. Thiruparankundram, Alagar Kovil, Pazhamudircholai and Koodal Azhagar also close for a few hours after midday. Timings change on festival days — our driver plans around them.

Dress code

Wear modest, traditional clothes with shoulders and knees covered — dhoti, veshti or trousers with a shirt for men; saree, churidar or salwar for women. Avoid shorts and sleeveless tops. Footwear is left outside the temple.

Phones & cameras

Mobile phones and cameras are not allowed inside the Meenakshi Amman Temple — deposit them at the counters near the entrance, or leave them safely in the car with the driver.

Madurai One Day Tour Package — Frequently Asked Questions

What places are covered in the Madurai one day tour package?
Meenakshi Amman Temple, Thirumalai Nayakkar Palace, Vandiyur Mariamman Teppakulam, Gandhi Memorial Museum, Koodal Azhagar Temple, Thiruparankundram Murugan Temple, and Alagar Kovil with Pazhamudircholai. In one day most travellers cover the city sights plus either Alagar Kovil and Pazhamudircholai or Thiruparankundram — you choose, and the driver adjusts the route.
What is the Madurai one day tour package price?
The tour runs on our 100 km/day plan: ₹3200 for a Dzire or equivalent, ₹4000 for an Ertiga, ₹4200 for an Innova or equivalent, ₹4900 for an Innova Crysta, ₹5700 for an Innova Hycross and ₹5200 for a 12+1 Tempo Traveller. Kilometres beyond 100 are charged at the extra per-km rate shown in the tariff. Toll, parking, permit, driver bata and hill charges are extra wherever applicable, and entry tickets are paid directly at each place.
Why is the 100 km/day plan right for a Madurai local tour?
A full day of Madurai sightseeing — the city temples, palace and museum plus Alagar Kovil or Thiruparankundram — usually adds up to about 80–100 km, so the 100 km plan covers it without paying for kilometres you won't use. If you add an airport pickup or extra places and go past 100 km, only the extra kilometres are charged per km.
How long does the Madurai one day sightseeing tour take?
About 8–10 hours. A typical day starts around 7 AM with the Meenakshi Amman Temple and ends between 5 and 6 PM. We can start earlier or finish later to suit your train, your flight or the evening temple ceremony.
Do you pick up from Madurai railway station, airport or bus stand?
Yes. We pick up and drop at any hotel or home in Madurai, Madurai Junction railway station, Madurai airport, and Mattuthavani or Periyar bus stand. Share your train or flight details and the driver meets you on arrival.
What are the Meenakshi Amman Temple timings?
The temple is open roughly 5 AM to 12:30 PM and 4 PM to 9:30 PM. The other temples on the tour also close for a few hours after midday, which is why we plan temples for the morning and late afternoon. Timings change on festival days.
Is there a dress code for Madurai temples?
Yes — wear modest, traditional clothes with shoulders and knees covered: dhoti, veshti or trousers with a shirt for men, and saree, churidar or salwar for women. Avoid shorts and sleeveless tops. Mobile phones and cameras are not allowed inside the Meenakshi Amman Temple.
Are entry tickets and guide charges included?
No. The package covers the A/C vehicle, the driver and fuel for the included 100 km. Entry tickets, camera fees, special darshan tickets, food and guide fees are paid directly by you.
Which car is best for a Madurai city tour?
A Dzire or equivalent suits couples and small families. Families of 5–7 usually pick an Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta or Innova Hycross; larger groups can take a Tempo Traveller, Force Urbania, van or mini bus.
Can I combine the Madurai day tour with Rameshwaram or Kodaikanal?
Yes. Many travellers add a day trip to Rameshwaram or Kodaikanal — see our Madurai to Rameshwaram tour package and Madurai to Kodaikanal taxi, or browse all Madurai tour packages for a multi-day plan.
