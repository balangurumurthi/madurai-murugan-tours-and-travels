Madurai to Kodaikanal Taxi — Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Madurai to Kodaikanal taxi fare?
The Madurai to Kodaikanal taxi fare follows our published tariff: 100 km or 200 km coverage per day from ₹3000/day for an Etios or Dzire up to ₹7500/day for an Innova Hycross, or km basis from ₹14/km with a per-day minimum. Toll, parking and hill charges are extra. Send your date on WhatsApp and we confirm the exact Madurai to Kodaikanal cab fare before you pay anything.
What is the Madurai to Kodaikanal distance by car?
The Madurai to Kodaikanal distance is about 120 km by road via Vadipatti and Batlagundu. A Madurai to Kodaikanal taxi takes approximately 3.5 to 4 hours — the last 45 km or so is the winding Kodaikanal ghat road climbing to about 2,100 m.
Can I do a Madurai to Kodaikanal one day trip by taxi?
Yes. Start from Madurai by 5:00–6:00 AM, cover Silver Cascade, Kodai Lake, Coaker's Walk, Bryant Park, Pillar Rocks and Guna Caves, and be back in Madurai by night. A Madurai to Kodaikanal one day trip runs to roughly 280–300 km, so the km-basis plan usually works out cheapest.
Is a Madurai to Kodaikanal 2 days tour package better than a one day trip?
If you can spare it, yes. With a night in Kodaikanal you avoid 7–8 hours of driving in one day, see the sunrise from Coaker's Walk, and add Upper Lake View, Moir Point, Pine Forest, Dolphin's Nose and Kurinji Andavar Temple without rushing. We arrange the hotel along with the cab.
Do you provide Madurai airport to Kodaikanal taxi pickup?
Yes, we provide Madurai airport to Kodaikanal taxi with name-placard pickup at the arrivals exit. Share your flight number on WhatsApp and the driver tracks your arrival time, so you head straight up to Kodaikanal.
Do you pick up from Madurai railway station for the Kodaikanal trip?
Yes. Madurai railway station (Madurai Junction) to Kodaikanal taxi and Mattuthavani or Arapalayam bus stand pickups are available. The driver waits at the exit with a name board.
Are your drivers experienced on the Kodaikanal ghat road?
Yes. Our drivers run the Madurai to Kodaikanal route regularly and know the hairpin bends, fog patches and safe stopping points. We plan the trip so the ghat-road descent is done before it gets dark.
Which car is best for a Madurai to Kodaikanal trip?
For a couple, an Etios or Dzire is the cheapest Madurai to Kodaikanal cab. On the ghat road, families usually prefer the extra power and space of an Innova, Innova Crysta or Innova Hycross. Groups can book a 12 or 18 seater Tempo Traveller.
Is a Madurai to Kodaikanal tempo traveller available for groups?
Yes — 12+1 and 18+1 seater A/C Tempo Travellers are available for Madurai to Kodaikanal group trips, college tours and family outings. Hotel rooms and food in Kodaikanal can be arranged for the whole group.
What places are covered in Kodaikanal sightseeing?
A typical Madurai to Kodaikanal taxi tour covers Silver Cascade Falls, Kodaikanal Lake, Bryant Park, Coaker's Walk, Pillar Rocks, Guna Caves, Pine Forest, Moir Point, Upper Lake View, Bear Shola Falls and Kurinji Andavar Temple. Berijam Lake needs a forest department permit on the day. Tell the driver what you want to cover — the route is flexible.
What is the best time to visit Kodaikanal from Madurai?
Kodaikanal is pleasant all year. April to June is peak season when Madurai is hottest, so book the cab early. September to November brings mist and greenery; December to February is cold, so carry woollens.
Is the Madurai to Kodaikanal cab fare inclusive of toll, parking and hill charges?
Toll, parking, Kodaikanal entry and hill charges and driver bata beyond the plan are extra, as on our standard tariff. We confirm the full Madurai to Kodaikanal taxi total on WhatsApp before your trip — no hidden charges after that.
Can I book a Kodaikanal to Madurai drop taxi?
Yes. We run Kodaikanal to Madurai taxi drops as well — from your hotel in Kodaikanal to Madurai city, Madurai airport or Madurai Junction. WhatsApp your pickup point and time for the fare.
How do I book a Madurai to Kodaikanal taxi?
Call +91 99946 67622 / +91 90879 67622 or use the WhatsApp booking form below. Tell us your date, pickup point and car type — we confirm the Madurai to Kodaikanal cab booking with the exact fare, usually within minutes.
