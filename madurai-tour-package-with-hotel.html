What's included in a tour package with hotel

Included

  • A/C vehicle with an experienced driver for every day of the trip
  • Hotel rooms for the nights you need, in the category you choose
  • Breakfast with the room, as per the hotel's plan
  • Lunch and dinner on request (veg or non-veg)
  • Pickup & drop — Madurai airport, railway station, bus stand or your address
  • Sightseeing as per the agreed day-by-day route
  • Route planning and one combined quote for cab + hotel + food

Not included

  • Toll, parking, permit, driver bata and hill charges — extra wherever applicable
  • Temple special-darshan, monument and park entry tickets
  • Boating, safari, adventure activities and guide fees
  • Meals not added to your plan
  • Personal expenses — room service, laundry, minibar, shopping
  • Early check-in / late check-out charges, as per hotel policy
  • Kilometres beyond the plan — charged at the extra-km rate

Choose your hotel category

We have direct tie-ups with 3-star and 5-star hotels in Madurai, Rameswaram and across Tamil Nadu — that means a better room rate than online booking sites. Pick the comfort level that suits your budget, or mix categories across the trip.

Budget

Clean, simple A/C rooms for travellers who spend the day sightseeing and only need a comfortable night's rest.

  • A/C room with attached bathroom
  • Convenient for temples and town centres
  • Keeps the overall package cost low
Good for: pilgrim groups, short stays, budget family trips

3-star

The most popular choice for family tour packages — more space and comfort, with in-house dining.

  • Comfortable rooms, family room options
  • Restaurant and breakfast on site
  • Direct tie-up rates where available
Good for: families, couples, senior citizens

5-star & premium

Luxury hotels and resorts for a relaxed holiday, special occasions or honeymoon trips.

  • Premium rooms and amenities
  • Resort options in hill stations and Kerala
  • Direct tie-up rates where available
Good for: honeymoon, anniversaries, premium holidays

Family tour packages from Madurai with accommodation

Most of our cab and hotel packages are booked by families. A family of four to six usually travels in an Innova Crysta, Innova Hycross or Innova, with family or connecting rooms and a breakfast plan. Couples often pick a Dzire or equivalent or an Ertiga and a better hotel or resort.

Tour Package with Hotel — Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in your Madurai tour package with hotel?
An A/C vehicle with an experienced driver for every day of the trip, hotel rooms for the nights you need in the category you choose (budget, 3-star or 5-star), breakfast as per the hotel's plan, and lunch or dinner on request. Pickup and drop from Madurai airport, railway station, bus stand or your address and sightseeing as per the agreed route are part of the plan. You get one quote for cab + hotel + food.
Why don't you show hotel names and room rates on the website?
Hotel room rates change with season, festival dates, weekends and availability, so a fixed price on a website would quickly be wrong. Instead we check availability for your exact dates and share hotel options with the current price in your quote on WhatsApp.
How is the price of a cab + hotel package calculated?
The car part follows our standard tariff — 100 km or 200 km coverage per day with an extra per-km rate, or km basis with a per-day minimum. Hotel rooms are added for the nights and category you choose, plus food if you want it. Toll, parking, permit, driver bata and hill charges are extra wherever applicable. Everything is combined into one quote before you pay anything.
Which hotel categories can I choose?
Budget hotels with clean A/C rooms, comfortable 3-star hotels, and 5-star or premium hotels and resorts. We have direct tie-ups with 3-star and 5-star hotels in Madurai, Rameswaram and across Tamil Nadu. You can also mix categories — for example budget in a temple town and a better hotel or resort in a hill station.
Can you arrange a Rameshwaram package with hotel?
Yes. A Rameshwaram package with hotel is one of our most requested plans — cab from Madurai, a night in Rameswaram so you can attend early-morning darshan at Ramanathaswamy Temple without rushing, and Dhanushkodi and Pamban Bridge sightseeing. It can be extended to Kanyakumari or Trivandrum.
Do you offer a Kodaikanal package with hotel and cab?
Yes. A Kodaikanal package with hotel and cab from Madurai usually has one or two nights in Kodaikanal, so you avoid driving up and down the ghat road in one day and can cover the lake, Coaker's Walk, Pillar Rocks, Guna Caves and the viewpoints comfortably.
Is food included in the package?
Food is optional. Breakfast comes with the room as per the hotel's plan, and lunch and dinner can be added on request — vegetarian or non-vegetarian. Tell us your preference when you share your plan.
Do you have family and group tour packages from Madurai with accommodation?
Yes. Families usually travel in an Innova, Innova Crysta or Innova Hycross with family or connecting rooms. Larger groups can take a Tempo Traveller, Force Urbania, 20 or 25 seater van or 33 seater mini bus, with rooms and food arranged for everyone.
How do I book a tour package with hotel stay?
Share your travel dates, number of people, places you want to visit and approximate budget using the form on this page, on WhatsApp or by phone. We suggest the route, car and hotel options and send one combined quote. Once you confirm, we book the cab and hotels for you.
